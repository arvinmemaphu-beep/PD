🌿 Halaman – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026
Buod (Summary in Filipino): Ang Halaman ay walang account at walang sariling server. Ang litrato ng halaman na pipiliin mo ay ipinapadala lang sa Google Gemini para ma-diagnose. Ang history at API key mo ay naka-save lang sa sarili mong device. Hindi kami nagbebenta ng data, walang ads, at walang tracking.

1. About the app

Halaman ("the app") is a free plant health helper. You take or choose a photo of a plant, and the app gives you an AI-generated diagnosis (healthy, not healthy, or dying) with suggestions on watering, sunlight, fertilizer and pest or disease treatment. In this policy, "we" and "the developer" mean the person who publishes Halaman: [YOUR NAME / DEVELOPER NAME].

2. Information the app handles

  • Plant photos. Only photos you choose to take with the camera or pick from your gallery. The app does not access your camera or gallery in the background.
  • Diagnosis results. The plant name, status and advice returned for a photo, saved as your history.
  • Gemini API key. The free API key you paste in the Setup tab.

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts, and it has no user accounts.

3. How your information is used

  • Photo → Google Gemini. When you tap "I-diagnose", the photo (resized and compressed on your device) is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API to be analyzed. The request uses the API key that you entered. The photo is not sent to any server owned by the developer.
  • History and API key → your device only. Results and your API key are stored in your device's local browser storage. They are never uploaded to the developer.

4. Third-party service: Google Gemini API

Photos you analyze are processed by Google under Google's own terms and privacy practices. Depending on your Gemini API plan, Google may retain submitted content and may use it to improve its services. Please read:

  • Gemini API Additional Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

Because the free API key is created and owned by you, your usage is also governed by your agreement with Google. Avoid photographing people or private documents, since the photo is sent to Google for analysis.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data.
  • We do not run advertising or analytics, and we do not track you.
  • We do not collect data from children knowingly. The app is a general gardening tool and is not directed to children under 13.

6. Data storage, retention and deletion

History (up to the 50 most recent results) and your API key stay on your device until you remove them:

  • Use the "I-clear lahat" button in the History tab to delete all saved history, or delete single entries.
  • Remove or replace your API key in the Setup tab.
  • Clearing the app's data or site data in your phone settings, or uninstalling the app, also removes everything stored on the device.

Since the developer does not receive or store your data, there is nothing on our side to delete. To remove photos already sent to Google, please refer to Google's policies linked above.

7. Security

Photos are sent to Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Your history and API key are kept in your device's local storage, so anyone who has access to your unlocked device or browser data may be able to see them. Keep your API key private.

8. Not professional advice

Diagnoses and recommendations are generated by AI and may be wrong. They are not a substitute for a professional agronomist, especially for serious plant problems.
